# Role research

Content source for **Phase 1: role catalog and role discovery**: a `/roles`
index, a `/roles/:slug` page per role, and the job request form linking to
roles by slug. Written for the owner (to decide what we place and how we
describe it) and the developer (to build the catalog data and pages).

The test for every entry: a US or Canadian business owner reads it and knows
what this person would actually do for their company.

## How this was researched

- **When:** October 2026.
- **Sources:** live job postings on OnlineJobs.ph, JobStreet PH, Indeed PH and
  LinkedIn (plus Workable, Lever and Remotive listings from PH agencies);
  competitor role pages (MyOutDesk, Cloudstaff, Penbrothers, Wing, Athena,
  HelpSquad, VA Masters, HireTalent.ph); vendor docs for the tools named; and,
  for the compliance section, regulator pages (FTC/FCC, CRTC, ACMA, state real
  estate commissions, BCFSA) and law-firm notes.
- **Method:** each role is written the way an operator would brief a
  recruiter: the recurring tasks, the tools, what changes with seniority, who
  buys it and what makes them buy.

> **Pay caveat.** Every pay figure here is indicative. Most come from
> individual employer postings (many dated January to September 2026, some
> already closed), not salary surveys. "PH pay" means what the worker is
> paid. Client rates (what a business pays an agency) run about 1.5x to 2.5x
> higher and are labelled "client rate" where they appear. HireTalent.ph
> guide figures are direct-hire marketplace rates and run high. Postings
> mostly convert at PHP 56-57 to USD 1 and 160-176 hours a month.
> **Do not publish any of these numbers until the owner confirms our own
> rates.**

## What competitors do with roles

- Most agencies show a grid of role tiles: a title, an icon and one generic
  sentence. Nothing tells a buyer what the person does on a Tuesday.
- Somewhere, Penbrothers and VirtualStaff publish a deep page per role
  (tasks, skills, tools, salary bands). These pages rank for searches like
  "hire a Filipino bookkeeper" and bring in buyers who already know the role
  they want.
- Rate guides (HireTalent.ph, VA Masters) publish per-role salary tables and
  win the "how much does a Filipino X cost" searches.
- Vertical specialists go deep on one industry and lead with compliance:
  MyOutDesk on real estate (ISA, TC), Winning Assistants and HelpSquad on
  healthcare (HIPAA, BAAs).
- Generalists such as Cloudstaff and Wing list many roles, including niche
  ones (Wing lists a "Corporate Travel Coordinator"), but mostly as titles.
- Our opening: specific tasks, named tools and a clear "what this role is
  not" on every page. Salary bands can follow once our rates are confirmed.

## 1. Administrative & Executive Support

Category slug: `admin-support`

### Executive Assistant

`executive-assistant` · **Launch**

**What they do:** Runs one owner's calendar, inbox and follow-ups so the owner
only spends time on decisions that need them.

**Common tasks**
- Triage the owner's inbox twice a day: archive noise, flag the 5-10 messages
  that need the owner, draft replies for approval.
- Own the calendar: book and reschedule, protect focus blocks, add a short
  prep note to each external meeting.
- Run a 15-30 minute daily check-in and turn the outcomes into tasks with
  owners and due dates in ClickUp or Asana.
- Keep a "waiting on" list and chase vendors, staff and clients until each
  item is closed.
- Write SOPs in Google Docs or Notion for anything the owner has explained
  twice.
- Book travel, file receipts and submit monthly expenses.
- Handle first-pass hiring admin: post the job, screen applicants against a
  rubric, schedule interviews.

**Skills:** clear written English (the first screen in most postings);
judgment about what to escalate; calendar discipline; drafting in the boss's
voice; daily use of AI writing tools.

**Tools:** Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, Calendly, Slack,
ClickUp/Asana/Notion, Loom, Zoom/RingCentral, ChatGPT/Claude, Expensify,
QuickBooks Online.

**Levels**
- Junior (1-2 yrs): follows the inbox and calendar rules the owner sets.
- Mid (3-5 yrs): writes those rules, drafts replies without templates, runs
  the weekly priorities list.
- Senior (5+ yrs): works as a chief of staff; runs projects, attends meetings
  for the owner, owns vendor relationships.

**Who hires and when:** Founders of 5-50 person service businesses (agencies,
clinic groups, home-care agencies, ecommerce brands). Trigger: the owner
spends 10+ hours a week on email and scheduling, or missed follow-ups are
costing deals.

**Not to be confused with:** Administrative Assistant (works a shared team
queue; the EA works for one person and makes judgment calls for them).
Project Coordinator (serves the whole team's delivery, not one person's
calendar).

**Indicative PH pay:** OnlineJobs.ph 2026 postings: PHP 30-40k (Jul 2026), PHP
36-42k + bonus (Canadian client), PHP 65k (May 2026), PHP 80k for 38 hrs (AU
client, Jan 2026), $8-15/hr (Texas home-care CEO, Aug 2026). JobStreet company
pages mostly PHP 20-35k, up to PHP 40-53k. HireTalent "Executive" $933-1,513/mo.

### Administrative Assistant

`administrative-assistant` · **Launch** · aliases: Admin & Inbox Assistant,
General Admin VA, Virtual Assistant, Data Entry Specialist

**What they do:** Works the shared admin queue: the info@ inbox, data entry,
document filing and simple templated replies to customers.

**Common tasks**
- Clear the shared info@ or support@ inbox each morning and afternoon; answer
  routine questions from saved replies and route the rest.
- Enter new leads, orders or client details from forms and emails into the
  CRM or spreadsheet the same day.
- Rename, file and tag documents in Google Drive or SharePoint to the folder
  standard.
- Prepare proposals, letters and onboarding packs from templates and send them
  for e-signature.
- Update the weekly KPI sheet from source systems every Monday.
- Renew subscriptions, order supplies, and keep the vendor and login list
  current in the password manager.

**Skills:** accuracy on repetitive entry; following an SOP exactly; plain
written English; spreadsheet basics; knowing when to ask.

**Tools:** Gmail/Outlook, Google Sheets/Excel, Google Drive/SharePoint,
DocuSign/PandaDoc, HubSpot or a simple CRM, Trello/Asana, 1Password/LastPass.

**Levels**
- Junior (0-2 yrs): works from SOPs.
- Mid (2-4 yrs): writes and fixes SOPs, can cover for an EA.
- Rarely senior: strong performers move into EA or project coordinator roles.

**Who hires and when:** 3-20 person offices (accounting firms, property
managers, insurance brokers, trades businesses). Trigger: the owner or office
manager is buried in admin and front-desk or billing work is slipping.

**Not to be confused with:** Email & Chat Support Rep (owns customer
conversations; this role handles back-office admin with only light,
templated customer contact). Executive Assistant (one person's priorities).

**Indicative PH pay:** VA Masters non-voice admin PHP 18-25k entry, 25-35k mid.
Indeed, Penbrothers Administrative Assistant about PHP 19.2k. HireTalent admin
$863-1,478/mo; data entry average $968/mo.

### Scheduling & Travel Coordinator

`scheduling-travel-coordinator` · Later

**What they do:** Books and juggles appointments, calendars and travel for a
team of several people (partners, field staff or clinicians).

**Common tasks**
- Book client appointments across 3-10 team calendars; send confirmations and
  day-before reminders.
- Rebook cancellations and fill gaps from a waitlist the same day.
- Build travel itineraries within policy and send one PDF per trip with all
  confirmation numbers.
- Track loyalty numbers, seat and hotel preferences, and passport expiry dates
  for everyone who travels.
- Coordinate multi-party meetings across time zones and post the agreed time
  with a video link.
- Match travel bookings to card statements each month and forward receipts to
  bookkeeping.

**Skills:** time-zone fluency; checking details (dates, names matching IDs);
fast rebooking under pressure; polite firmness with outside parties.

**Tools:** Google Calendar/Outlook, Calendly/Acuity, Navan/Expedia/Google
Flights, airline and hotel portals, practice schedulers (Jane, SimplePractice,
ServiceTitan), Slack.

**Levels**
- Junior (1-2 yrs): appointment booking.
- Mid (3+ yrs): executive and international travel, rebooking during
  disruptions, managing a travel budget.

**Who hires and when:** Multi-practitioner clinics, consulting and law firms
with travelling partners, event and sales teams. Trigger: someone internal
spends half their week on bookings, or a time-zone mix-up causes a missed
meeting.

**Not to be confused with:** Executive Assistant (priorities and
correspondence for one person; this role owns logistics for many).

**Indicative PH pay:** No role-specific data found. Use the admin band as a
proxy: VA Masters non-voice PHP 18-35k; HireTalent admin average $1,215/mo.

## 2. Finance & Accounting

Category slug: `finance-accounting`

### Bookkeeper

`bookkeeper` · **Launch** · aliases: QuickBooks Bookkeeper, Xero Bookkeeper

**What they do:** Keeps the books categorized, reconciled and ready for the
accountant every month.

**Common tasks**
- Categorize bank and card transactions in QuickBooks Online or Xero daily or
  weekly, and maintain bank rules.
- Reconcile bank, credit card, merchant (Stripe, Square, Shopify Payments) and
  loan accounts monthly.
- Post month-end entries: depreciation, prepaid amortization, payroll
  accruals.
- Chase missing receipts and attach them through Dext or Hubdoc.
- Close the month by a set date (for example the 10th) and send the P&L,
  balance sheet and a short variance note to the owner.
- Prepare the year-end pack and answer the CPA's questions.
- Catch up books for new clients who are months behind.

**Skills:** double-entry accounting; reconciliation discipline; US GAAP
basics; spotting miscategorized transactions; writing clear notes for owners
who are not accountants.

**Tools:** QuickBooks Online, Xero, Dext, Hubdoc, Excel/Google Sheets,
Stripe and Shopify reports, Bill.com, Karbon/Financial Cents (firm workflow).
QBO ProAdvisor or Xero Advisor certification is commonly listed.

**Levels**
- Junior (1-2 yrs): categorization and reconciliations under review.
- Mid (3+ yrs, multi-client): owns the month-end close for 5-15 clients.
- Senior: reviews other bookkeepers' work; runs catch-up and clean-up projects.

**Who hires and when:** Small business owners whose books are 3+ months behind
before tax season. CPA and bookkeeping firms adding offshore capacity (a very
common buyer).

**Not to be confused with:** Staff Accountant (accruals, complex adjustments
and financial statements under a CPA; the bookkeeper records and reconciles).

**Indicative PH pay:** OnlineJobs.ph, ClarityCFO (Aug 2026, 3 yrs,
multi-client) PHP 40k. Indeed, OVA Virtual about PHP 40k (17 postings). MYCPE
ONE posting PHP 40-61k. Client rates: HireTalent $1,056-1,602/mo; HireTalent
survey $10-12/hr basic, $20-30/hr certified QBO/Xero.

### Accounts Payable Specialist

`accounts-payable-specialist` · **Launch** · aliases: AP Specialist, Accounts
Payable / Receivable Specialist

**What they do:** Gets vendor bills entered, approved and paid on time,
without duplicates or fraud.

**Common tasks**
- Enter incoming vendor invoices daily from the AP inbox into Bill.com, QBO or
  Xero with the right GL code and class.
- Match invoices to purchase orders and receiving documents; hold mismatches.
- Route bills for approval and chase approvers before the payment run.
- Prepare the weekly payment run and send it for sign-off.
- Reconcile vendor statements monthly; resolve short-pays and credits.
- Keep vendor records current (W-9s, bank details) and verify any bank-detail
  change by phone.
- Track 1099 vendors for year-end.

**Skills:** invoice accuracy; three-way matching; fraud awareness on
bank-detail changes; handling volume against deadlines; vendor email
etiquette.

**Tools:** Bill.com, QuickBooks Online, Xero, NetSuite, Ramp/Brex, Dext,
Excel. (AU clients: Xero plus ApprovalMax.)

**Levels**
- Junior (1-2 yrs): entry and matching.
- Mid: owns the payment run and vendor reconciliations.
- Senior: AP lead; manages the approval workflow and month-end AP accruals
  (one posting cited about 2,300 invoices processed).

**Who hires and when:** Multi-location businesses (restaurants, construction,
wholesale) with 200+ bills a month. Trigger: late-payment fees, or a
controller doing data entry.

**Not to be confused with:** Bookkeeper (reconciliations and month-end close;
AP handles one side of the ledger at volume). Accounts Receivable Specialist
(money coming in, not going out).

**Indicative PH pay:** Cloudstaff AP Officer (entry) PHP 30k. Cloudstaff AP
Specialist PHP 35-45k (expired listing). VA Masters accounting band PHP
20-50k entry to mid.

### Accounts Receivable & Collections Specialist

`accounts-receivable-specialist` · **Launch** · aliases: AR Specialist,
Collections Specialist, Billing Specialist

**What they do:** Turns finished work into invoices and invoices into cash by
billing promptly and chasing overdue accounts.

**Common tasks**
- Raise invoices from approved service tickets, completed jobs and estimates
  within 24 hours of completion.
- Check each invoice for rates, quantities, PO references and sales tax
  before it goes out.
- Work the AR aging weekly by 30/60/90+ bucket with an email-then-phone
  follow-up cadence.
- Apply incoming payments, issue receipts, clear unapplied cash.
- Compare the job schedule to invoices to find completed but unbilled work,
  and flag it to operations.
- Send a weekly AR report with at-risk accounts escalated to the owner.
- Prepare sales tax data for the bookkeeper to file.

**Skills:** firm but polite collections calls and emails; invoice accuracy;
aging management; working with dispatch and project managers; basic sales tax
knowledge.

**Tools:** QuickBooks Online, Xero, ServiceTitan/Jobber (job-based
businesses), Stripe, Chaser/Upflow, NetSuite, Excel.

**Levels**
- Junior: raises invoices and applies payments.
- Mid (2+ yrs): owns collections calls and the unbilled-work audit.
- Senior: credit decisions and payment plans (postings often keep pricing and
  terms out of scope).

**Who hires and when:** Service and project businesses (trades, IT
integrators, B2B agencies) with $50k+ outstanding past 60 days. Trigger: cash
is tight and the owner is the one chasing.

**Not to be confused with:** Email & Chat Support Rep (AR talks to customers
only about money owed). Medical Billing Specialist (insurance claims, a
different system).

**Indicative PH pay:** OnlineJobs.ph, Structured Progress LLC (Feb 2026, 2+
yrs) $1,000-1,600/mo (stated as PHP 60-85k). OnlineJobs.ph, WA company (Feb
2026) PHP 40k. JobStreet Taguig AR Specialist PHP 55-65k. Payscale early-career
collections about PHP 30k.

### Payroll Specialist

`payroll-specialist` · Later

**What they do:** Runs payroll accurately and on time and files the related
tax and benefit obligations.

**Common tasks**
- Collect timesheets and changes (new hires, terminations, raises, leave) by
  cut-off and enter them in Gusto or ADP.
- Run weekly, biweekly or monthly pay cycles and send the pre-run register
  for approval.
- Reconcile federal and state withholding and benefit deductions; check
  941 and W-2 data.
- Answer employee pay questions within one business day.
- Reconcile payroll clearing accounts to the general ledger monthly.
- Prepare year-end W-2 data.

**Skills:** country-specific payroll law (FLSA and state rules); confidentiality;
reconciliation; deadline reliability; explaining pay to employees.

**Tools:** Gusto, ADP Workforce Now/RUN, Paychex, QuickBooks Payroll, Paycor.
(AU postings: Xero Payroll, MYOB, Employment Hero, Deputy.)

**Levels**
- Junior: prepares inputs under review.
- Mid (3+ yrs): owns end-to-end runs for single-state clients.
- Senior (6+ yrs): multi-state payroll tax, reviews others' runs, mentors.

**Who hires and when:** Accounting firms offering payroll to clients; 30-200
employee businesses with multi-state complexity. Trigger: a payroll error
that led to back pay or an IRS notice, or the office manager who ran payroll
quit.

**Not to be confused with:** HR & Payroll Admin Assistant (prepares payroll
inputs and handles HR paperwork inside one company; this role runs payroll as
a specialist, often for many clients). Not HR: no hiring, performance or
employee relations.

**Indicative PH pay:** OnlineJobs.ph, AU accounting firm (Sep 2026, 6+ yrs AU
payroll) PHP 60-80k. JobStreet remote AU payroll PHP 65-85k. Most PH payroll
postings found were AU-focused; US and Canadian payroll pay was not found
separately.

### Staff Accountant

`staff-accountant` · **Launch** · aliases: Accountant, Senior Accountant

**What they do:** Handles the work above bookkeeping (accruals, balance-sheet
reconciliations, financial statements) under a CPA or controller.

**Common tasks**
- Prepare month-end accruals, prepaid and deferred revenue schedules, and
  fixed-asset schedules.
- Reconcile every balance-sheet account monthly with support attached.
- Prepare the month-end package (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, variance
  analysis) for controller review.
- Prepare audit and tax schedules and answer auditor requests.
- Post intercompany and multi-entity consolidation entries.
- Review junior bookkeepers' reconciliations before close.

**Skills:** accrual accounting and US GAAP; balance-sheet reconciliations;
Excel (lookups, pivots); audit readiness; written explanations of variances.
A US CPA or PH CPA board passer is common.

**Tools:** QuickBooks Online (required in many postings), Xero, NetSuite,
Sage Intacct, FloQast, Excel.

**Levels**
- Mid (2-4 yrs): owns the close for smaller entities.
- Senior (4+ yrs): year-end close and audit support for US clients.
- Always needs a CPA or controller above it.

**Who hires and when:** Companies at $3M+ revenue with an outsourced CFO or
fractional controller; US CPA firms in audit and tax season. Trigger: the
controller is doing close work, or audit prep is running late.

**Not to be confused with:** Bookkeeper (keeps transactions right; the staff
accountant makes period-end numbers right and defensible). Not a CPA who
signs off or gives tax advice.

**Indicative PH pay:** VA Masters accounting senior band PHP 50-80k. Indeed,
MySigrid Accountant about PHP 41.9k (n=2). Client rate: VA Masters staff
accountant $9-13/hr.

## 3. Customer Support

Category slug: `customer-support`

### Email & Chat Support Rep

`email-chat-support-rep` · **Launch** · aliases: Customer Support
Representative, Ecommerce Customer Service, Order Ops

Merged from two research entries (file 1 "Email & Chat Support Rep
(E-commerce)" and file 2 "Ecommerce Customer Service & Order Ops"), which
described the same job. The Ecommerce category links here rather than
repeating it.

**What they do:** Answers customer emails, chats and DMs about orders,
shipping and returns, and fixes the order problems behind them.

**Common tasks**
- Work the Gorgias or Zendesk queue to a response target (for example under 2
  hours on email, under 2 minutes on chat).
- Look up orders in Shopify and handle where-is-my-order, address changes,
  swaps and cancellations before shipping.
- Process returns, exchanges and refunds within the refund policy and dollar
  limit.
- Chase carriers and the 3PL on lost or damaged packages; file claims and
  reship.
- Answer PayPal, Stripe and Shopify Payments chargebacks with evidence.
- Reply to Instagram and Facebook comments and DMs; hide spam.
- Tag every ticket by reason and send a weekly top-reasons summary.
- Update macros and help-center articles when a policy changes.

**Skills:** fast, warm written English; policy judgment (refund or replace);
Shopify order handling; ticket tagging discipline; staying calm with angry
customers.

**Tools:** Gorgias, Zendesk, Help Scout, Re:amaze, Richpanel, Shopify, Loop
Returns, AfterShip, ShipStation, ShipBob, Route, Meta Business Suite.

**Levels**
- Junior (0-1 yr, often ex-BPO): works the queue from macros, escalates
  anything off-script.
- Mid (2-3 yrs): handles refunds, disputes and escalations alone; writes
  macros.
- Senior (5+ yrs): CX lead; administers the helpdesk, owns response targets
  and help-center content, manages a small team.

**Who hires and when:** Shopify brands doing $1M-20M a year and multi-channel
sellers. Trigger: the founder answering tickets at midnight, or a Black
Friday or holiday spike.

**Not to be confused with:** Technical Support Specialist (troubleshoots a
software product). Not a warehouse role: they do not pack orders.

**Indicative PH pay:** JobStreet Gorgias support (part-time, about 20 hrs)
PHP 30-40k. VA Masters non-voice PHP 18-35k. Gorgias/Zendesk profiles $5-8/hr
on Hubstaff Talent and HireTalent. HireTalent customer support $851-1,250/mo.
Armasourcing CS median ask $800/mo.

### Phone CSR & Dispatcher (Home Services)

`phone-csr-dispatcher` · **Launch** · aliases: Customer Service
Representative (voice), Call Center Agent, Home Services Dispatcher

**What they do:** Answers inbound calls, books jobs and keeps the technicians'
schedule full for a trades business.

**Common tasks**
- Answer inbound calls and web leads, qualify the job (system type, issue,
  address, membership) and book it in ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro.
- Adjust the dispatch board when techs run long or emergencies come in; text
  customers updated arrival times.
- Confirm the next day's appointments every afternoon by call or SMS.
- Call members about maintenance-agreement renewals, open estimates and
  review requests.
- Take card payments by phone and send invoices and financing links.
- Log every call with an outcome code and report booking rate weekly.

**Skills:** clear spoken English that US callers understand easily; call
control; scheduling under pressure; basic HVAC, plumbing and electrical
vocabulary; offering memberships without sounding pushy.

**Tools:** ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, RingCentral/Dialpad, Podium,
Google Local Services, CallRail.

**Levels**
- Junior (BPO voice background): answers and books.
- Mid (2+ yrs US dispatch): owns the dispatch board for 5-20 techs.
- Senior: CSR or dispatch lead; manages the call team and coaches booking
  rate.

**Who hires and when:** HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest control and
garage-door companies with 5-30 trucks. Trigger: missed calls turning into
lost jobs, or the office manager also dispatching.

**Not to be confused with:** Appointment Setter (cold outbound calls to create
demand; the CSR books inbound demand).

**Compliance:** outbound renewal and estimate follow-up calls fall under
calling rules. See [Calling and texting](#calling-and-texting).

**Indicative PH pay:** Remote Raven Dispatcher/CSR (ServiceTitan) $12,480/yr
(about $1,040/mo); another listing up to $6/hr. VA Masters voice PHP 15-25k
entry, 25-40k mid, 40-60k senior.

### Technical Support Specialist (SaaS)

`technical-support-specialist` · **Launch** · aliases: Product Support
Specialist, Tier 1 Support, Tier 2 Support

**What they do:** Answers software customers' "how do I" and "it's broken"
tickets, reproduces bugs and hands clean reports to engineering.

## Compliance and scope limits

Operator-level notes, not legal advice. Anything marked **verify before
publishing** came from general knowledge in the research, not a cited source,
and needs a check with counsel before it goes on the site.

### Healthcare (US)

- **HIPAA applies wherever the worker sits.** A VA who touches patient data
  (PHI) acts as a business associate, or as the agency's subcontractor. A
  Business Associate Agreement (BAA) must be signed **before** any PHI
  access. It should cover permitted uses, safeguards, breach reporting and
  flow-down to subcontractors. (AccountableHQ, HelpSquad, HireTalent BAA
  guide)
- Common practice: access only through a VDI or the EHR's web portal, no
  local downloads, unique logins with MFA, clean-desk and no-phone rules,
  HIPAA training certificates on file.
- The Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) also applies on the worker side;
  health data is sensitive personal information under it.
- **Verify before publishing:** some payer and government contracts restrict
  offshore PHI access. CMS requires Medicare Advantage and Part D plans to
  report offshore subcontractors, and some state Medicaid programs prohibit
  offshore access. Practices billing under those contracts should check
  before assigning offshore staff.
- Scope: medical VAs do not give clinical advice, triage symptoms or make
  prescribing decisions. Refill requests are routed, not approved.
- Canadian provincial health privacy laws were not researched.

### Real estate licensing

- Offshore staff are **unlicensed assistants**. Generally allowed: answering
  phones and taking messages, scheduling, MLS entry from broker-supplied
  data, assembling files, typing forms from broker-supplied information,
  marketing with broker approval. (NC Real Estate Commission, Georgia
  guidance, California DRE)
- Generally not allowed: showing property, hosting open houses, negotiating,
  explaining contract terms, financing or title to clients, quoting
  commission, giving pricing opinions, presenting offers. (NCREC, Texas,
  Georgia, BCFSA)
- **Cold calling to find listings is restricted in some states.** Georgia
  bars unlicensed assistants from soliciting prospects; Texas lets unlicensed
  staff schedule showings but not solicit business. MyOutDesk's own
  comparison marks ISA licensing as "state-dependent". An ISA must be scoped
  per state: scripts that set appointments for a licensed agent, no pricing
  or commission talk.
- NAR's state-by-state chart of unlicensed assistant rules was last reviewed
  in April 2020. Check each state commission.
- **Canada:** BCFSA publishes unlicensed assistant guidelines with similar
  limits (no negotiating, showing or presenting offers). Other provinces were
  not researched.
- **Property management:** the assistant can send notices and process
  applications; lease signing, approvals and fair-housing-sensitive decisions
  stay with the licensed property manager.
- **Australia, verify before publishing:** property and leasing work requires
  state licensing or registration (for example the NSW certificate of
  registration). Offshore PM assistants do back-office work only.

### Calling and texting

Applies to Appointment Setter, ISA, SDR (phone), Phone CSR (outbound
follow-ups) and Medical VA (reminder calls and texts).

- **US:** the National Do Not Call Registry and the TCPA apply to calls into
  the US wherever the caller is. The FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule limits
  calls to 8am-9pm in the recipient's local time and restricts abandoned
  calls. Keep consent records. Autodialed or prerecorded marketing calls and
  texts to cell phones need prior express written consent.
- The FCC "one-to-one consent" rule was vacated by the Eleventh Circuit in
  January 2025 (*Insurance Marketing Coalition v. FCC*). The FCC's "revoke
  all" consent-revocation rule is delayed to January 31, 2027. (MoFo, Perkins
  Coie, TCPA World)
- Pending, not law: the FCC proposed offshore call-center rules in March 2026
  (aimed at providers' customer service calls), and the Senate passed the
  Foreign Robocall Elimination Act in August 2026; it still needs the House.
  **Verify status before publishing.**
- **Verify before publishing:** several states have stricter "mini-TCPA"
  laws (Florida FTSA, Oklahoma, Maryland, Washington), narrower calling hours
  and their own DNC lists. Dialer setups should scrub against national, state
  and internal DNC lists and honor opt-outs right away.
- **Canada:** telemarketers calling Canadians must register with the
  National DNCL and subscribe for the area codes they call, whether based in
  Canada or abroad. Canadian companies are liable for their offshore agents.
  Calling hours: 9:00am-9:30pm weekdays, 10:00am-6:00pm weekends, recipient's
  local time. (CRTC)
- **Australia:** the Do Not Call Register Act applies to Australian
  businesses using overseas telemarketers. Calling hours: 9am-8pm weekdays,
  9am-5pm Saturday, no Sundays or national public holidays. Express
  permission lapses after 3 months. The Spam Act covers marketing SMS and
  email (consent, sender ID, working unsubscribe). Penalties have reached
  A$1.5M. (ACMA)
- Suggested position for role pages: the client owns the lead source,
  consent records and DNC scrubbing; our staff follow the client's compliant
  script and dialer setup, and honor opt-outs immediately.

### Recruiting and HR

- **Verify before publishing:** US background checks fall under the FCRA
  (disclosure and adverse-action steps), and many states and cities have
  ban-the-box and pay-transparency rules for job ads. An offshore recruiter
  should use templates approved by the client's US counsel.
- **Verify before publishing:** offshore HR assistants should not examine I-9
  documents unless the employer uses E-Verify's remote alternative procedure.
  A US-accountable person should own I-9 compliance.
- Employee SSNs, bank details and medical leave information are sensitive.
  Give offshore staff the minimum role in Gusto or ADP.

### Data privacy (all categories)

- **Verify before publishing:** Canada's PIPEDA and Australia's Privacy Act
  (APP 8, cross-border disclosure) hold the business accountable for personal
  data handled offshore. The PH Data Privacy Act applies on the worker side.
- Many PH roles are posted as independent contractors (W-8BEN for US tax).
  Agencies that employ staff locally carry 13th-month pay and statutory
  contributions (Hivedesk estimates about 22% on top of base).

### d. Copy rules for role pages

1. **Lead with tasks, not adjectives.** "Reconciles your bank and card
   accounts by the 10th" beats "detail-oriented finance professional".
2. **Name the tools.** Buyers search for "QuickBooks bookkeeper" and
   "ServiceTitan dispatcher". Tool names show we know the job.
3. **Say what the role is not.** One line on the neighboring role saves a
   mismatched hire and a bad first month.
4. **Name the trigger.** Open with the moment the buyer recognizes ("You're
   answering tickets at midnight").
5. **No unconfirmed numbers.** No pay, savings percentages or "hire in X
   days" until the owner confirms them. Keep research figures in this doc.
6. **Scope notes in plain words.** Where compliance applies, say what the
   person will and won't do ("Does not negotiate or give pricing opinions"),
   not a block of legal text.

- Software developers and UI/UX designers: not researched (open decision
  above).
- No role-specific PH pay for Scheduling & Travel Coordinator or Lead
  Research Specialist (admin and data-entry bands used as proxies).
  Recruiting Coordinator pay is one data point.

  rules), provincial real estate rules beyond BC, and provincial health
  privacy were not researched. Payroll postings found were mostly
  AU-focused.

  possibly closed. The ConnectOS 2026 salary brochure could not be read.
- Pay varies by client country and shift: US night-shift roles often add a
  night differential and HMO on top.

  service entries became one role, and Social Media Content Assistant became
  the junior level of Social Media Manager.
